1. OVERVIEW. The Meta VR Start Developer Competition: (“ Contest ”) is sponsored by Meta Platforms, Inc., 1 Hacker Way, Menlo Park, CA 94025 (“ Sponsor ”), and encourages developers to develop games and applications for Sponsor’s virtual immersive platform for Meta VR devices. Eligible entrants can enter this Contest as an individual, Team, or Organization (defined below) during the Entry Period. These Official Rules (“Official Rules”) govern participation in the Contest. Read through these entire Official Rules carefully to make sure you understand the details.
2. ELIGIBILITY. Entry and participation in this Contest is open only to individuals, Teams, and Organizations as defined below (each an “ Entrant ”). As of the start of the Entry Period (as defined above), all Entrants whether as an individual or member of a Team or Organization must:
(a) be at least eighteen years of age and the age of majority in their jurisdiction of residence;
(b) not be residents of, or Organizations domiciled in, Brazil, Quebec, or in any area subject to comprehensive international sanctions such as Crimea, Russia, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, or any other country or individual designated by the United States Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control;
(c) have an active and valid Meta account with Developer Access enabled or in the case of a Team or Organization member has administrative control over by the time of submission; and
(d) Representative or person submitting an Entry must be a member of the Meta VR Start Program by the time of submission.
Organizations (including corporations, not-for-profit corporations and other nonprofit organizations, limited liability companies, partnerships, and other legal entities) that exist and have been organized or incorporated at the time of entry are eligible to participate in the Contest referred to herein as an “ Organization ”. Organizations that are politically affiliated (as determined by Sponsor in its sole discretion) are not eligible to participate in the Contest. An association of at least two (2) individuals that does not constitute a legally recognized entity, provided that each natural person comprising such association and meets the eligibility criteria listed above, can submit Entries and are referred to herein as a “ Team ”.
Teams and Organizations must appoint and authorize one individual (the “ Representative ”) to represent, act, receive communications, participate, and receive any prize (if awarded), on behalf of the Team or Organization. By entering on behalf of a Team or Organization, each Representative represents and warrants on behalf of their Team or Organization that they are authorized to act on behalf of their Team or Organization. Sponsor shall have no liability for the acts or omissions of any Representative who acts on behalf of a Team or Organization.
Entrants may not be under any legal or contractual obligation that would prohibit their participation in the Contest. Any individual for whom participation in this Contest would mean a violation by them of any applicable law or of any agreement to which they are a party is ineligible to participate. Government and political officials, and employees, officers, directors, members, managers, agents, and representatives of Sponsor, or any other entities participating in the design, promotion, marketing, administration, or fulfillment of this Contest, or any of their respective corporate partners, parent companies, divisions, subsidiaries, affiliates, successors in interest, and advertising, promotion, and public relations agencies and any family (i.e., spouse, partner, parent, legal guardian, child, sibling, grandparent, grandchild, or in-law) or other member of the same household (whether or not related) (“ Released Parties ”) are also not eligible to enter or win this Contest. Any other individual or Organization whose participation in the Contest would create, in the sole discretion of the Sponsor, a real or apparent conflict of interest are not eligible to enter or win this Contest. Accordingly, each Team or Organization may submit only one (1) Entry, submitted by its Team Representative.
Participation in this Contest constitutes each Entrant’s full and unconditional agreement to and acceptance of these Official Rules and the decisions of Sponsor, which are final and binding in all matters.
Entry Limit. Each individual is limited to submitting one (1) Entry in this Contest. For the avoidance of doubt, an Entry is submitted either (i) by an individual on their own behalf, or (ii) by a Team Representative on behalf of a Team or Organization. Participation as a non-Representative Team member does not constitute submitting an Entry.
Permitted Participation Scenarios. Subject to the one-Entry limit above, an individual may participate in this Contest in any of the following ways:
(i) as a solo entrant, submitting one Entry on their own behalf;
(ii) as a solo entrant who submits one Entry on their own behalf and also participates as a non-Representative member of one or more Teams or Organizations (because non-Representative membership does not count as an Entry); or
(iii) as a Team Representative, submitting one Entry on that Team or Organization’s behalf (and optionally also serving as a non-Representative member of other Teams or Organizations).
Prohibition. An individual may not both submit an Entry as a solo entrant and serve as a Team Representative, as this would result in two Entries being attributed to that individual. Each individual may serve as the Representative of only one (1) Team or Organization.

All Entries, and elements thereof, must comply with the Entry Requirements listed in Section 4 (Entry Requirements) below to be considered for this Contest.

4. ENTRY REQUIREMENTS. Each Entry must meet all of the following requirements, as determined by Sponsor in its sole discretion, or the Entry may be disqualified:
-
Project: Include a Project that meets the following requirements.
-
App Type & Platform: The Project must be a VR/MR application built for Meta VR devices
-
Track: Must align with at least one of the following tracks (each a “Track”). At any time, and at its sole discretion, the Sponsor will have the ability to re-assign a submitted Entry from one Track to another.
-
Entertainment: Lean-back media and content experiences—spatial cinema, interactive video, music visualization, immersive storytelling, spatial audio, media companion apps (e.g., commentary tracks, popup production notes, social comments). Content designed to be consumed in any environment. Content designed to be consumed in any environment. Hands-first implementation required.
-
Gaming: Games designed for hands-first (required) or eyes and hands. Puzzle, strategy, casual, social, narrative—genres that thrive seated, without controllers.
-
Productivity: Apps that make you more effective anywhere or are designed around specific daily moments—multi-panel workspaces, task management, creative tools, or habits tied to a recurring context (morning routine, commute, wind-down). Hands-first implementation required.
-
Division: Projects must align with one of the following divisions (each a “Division”). At any time, and at its sole discretion, the Sponsor will have the ability to re-assign a submitted Entry from one Division to another.
- New Experience: Project is conceived of and built within the competition window (starting September 24, 2026). No pre-existing codebases, no shipped titles, no early access builds repurposed
- Adapted / Significantly Updated Experience: Pre-existing project with a meaningful new feature, mode, or platform integration shipped during the competition window. Any build, prototype, or published app that existed prior to September 24, 2026. Sponsor will determine in its own discretion if an update qualifies as significant.
- Examples of Significant Updates: include but are not limited to
- Implementing Hand Interactions as a new feature
- Adding multiplayer support
- Introducing a mixed reality (MR) interaction mode as a new feature
- Releasing a new user generated content creation tool
- Non-Examples: include but are not limited to
- Bug fixes or minor User Interface (UI) tweaks
- Cosmetic changes (e.g., new color scheme)
- Performance optimization without new user-facing features

5. WINNER SELECTION/NOTIFICATION. Entries will be evaluated based on the Judging criteria listed below by a panel of experts designated by Sponsor (“Judge(s)”) during the Judging Period. Judges may be employees of the Sponsor or third parties, may or may not be listed individually on the Competition website, and may change before or during the Judging Period. Judging may take place in one or more rounds with one or more panels of Judges, at the discretion of the Sponsor. AI tools may also be used to assist in the judging, but final decisions will be made by human judges.
Stage One: The first stage will determine via pass/fail whether the Projects meet a baseline level of viability, in that the Project and Entry reasonably fits the theme and reasonably applies the required tools and features featured in the Contest.
Stage Two: All Entries that pass Stage One will be evaluated in Stage Two based on the below equally weighted criteria (the “Judging Criteria”).
The scores from the Judges will determine the potential winners of the applicable prizes. The Entrant(s) that are eligible for a Prize, and whose Entries earn the highest overall scores based on the applicable Judging Criteria, will become potential winners of that Prize.

Measures originality, creative ambition, and relevance to the chosen track (Gaming, Entertainment, or Productivity).

Experience Design
Evaluates design, mechanics, and user journey for the intended audience, with emphasis on seated, hands-first, or context-specific use. Strong entries have intuitive navigation, clear onboarding, and a habit-forming purpose.
Passthrough should be purposeful: the player's real room meaningfully changes the experience, not a scene that plays the same with passthrough off.
We'll also evaluate FoV-aware design. Since the field of view differs between Meta VR devices, keep essential UI and interactions within a comfortable, narrower FoV and adapt across devices.
25%
Technical Implementation
Assesses technical expertise, reliability, and strategic use of platform tools, particularly the features that define hands-first experiences: gaze interactions (Unity/Unreal ISDK v207+), hand tracking, passthrough, spatial anchoring, and FoV-aware design.
High-scoring entries must be performant on Meta Quest hardware (min 60 fps ), solid and bug-free, and show seamless integration of features relevant to their specific track.
For entries that use passthrough, this includes correct use of scene understanding (MRUK), anchoring content to detected planes, surfaces, and room geometry, and holding up in rooms the developer never tested.

Assesses the overall quality of the project submission, including UI/UX, art direction, and sound design.
